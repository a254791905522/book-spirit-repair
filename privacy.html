Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Book Spirit Repair is a single-player offline puzzle game. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data Collection

Book Spirit Repair does not collect personal data. The game does not include account registration, social login, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases.

Local Progress

The app may save gameplay progress, unlocked chapters, and settings locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Internet and Third Parties

Book Spirit Repair is designed for offline play. It does not require an internet connection for gameplay and does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or user accounts.

Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

After release, please contact support through the App Store product page for Book Spirit Repair.
